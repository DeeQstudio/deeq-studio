05 / HOW IT WORKS

A clear process.
Less client homework.

You do not need a polished brief or a folder full of brand jargon. Bring the problem, the ambition or the thing that keeps getting postponed. We turn that into a workable next step.

  1. 01

    Bring the rough version.

    A call, an email, an old site, a half-finished idea. We help define what actually needs to be solved before anything gets designed.

  2. 02

    Direction before decoration.

    Structure, visual direction and priorities are made visible early. You know what we are building and why before the heavy production starts.

  3. 03

    Build in the open.

    You see real progress, not a mystery reveal at the end. Feedback is handled while changes are still cheap and easy to make.

  4. 04

    QA like it is already live.

    Mobile, desktop, keyboard, motion preferences, images, links, metadata and edge cases are part of the build, not an afterthought on launch day.

  5. 05

    Hand over, or hand it over to us.

    Your domain, code and accounts stay yours. Take the keys with a clean handoff, or keep DeeQ around for maintenance, content and media.

07 / BEFORE YOU ASK

The useful questions.

Do I need a complete brief before I contact you?+

No. A few sentences are enough. Tell us what you have, what is not working and what you would like to change. Part of the job is turning that into a clear scope.

Can DeeQ take over an existing website or identity?+

Yes. We can improve what is there, rebuild what needs rebuilding or work inside an existing identity. A redesign does not automatically mean throwing away everything that already works.

Will I be able to update the site myself?+

If that is how you want to work, yes. The technical setup should fit the people maintaining it. Easy content should stay easy to edit; the complex parts can stay safely out of the way.

What happens after launch?+

You choose. We can hand over the project cleanly, stay available for occasional changes, or take on ongoing maintenance and media work so it stops living on your to-do list.

What does a project cost?+

It depends on what the project actually needs. A focused company site and a custom platform are different jobs. After the initial conversation, you get a clear scope and price before work begins, including what is and is not part of it.
