THE JOB

One collective.
Three entrances.

Kwartier West needed a site that could hold different scenes without flattening them into one generic visual system.

The public structure starts with a choice: Tekno, Villa West or Hip hop. DeeQ uses that split as the interaction model, while booking and collective information stay coherent underneath.

What DeeQ handled

Experience direction, responsive interface design, front-end development, visual transitions, booking structure and the production layer around launch.
