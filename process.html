How it works · DeeQ Studio

Less mystery.
More progress.

A senior process should reduce the amount of project management the client has to do, not create more of it.

THE PROCESS

Clear enough to
feel easy.

You do not need a polished brief, a technical vocabulary or a finished answer before the first conversation.

01 · Bring the rough version

The existing site, the idea, the bottleneck or the goal is enough to start. The first job is defining the actual problem.

02 · Direction before production

Structure, priorities and visual direction become tangible before the heavy build, so big decisions are made while they are still cheap to change.

03 · Build in the open

You see real progress and can react to real screens. No mystery reveal after weeks of silence.

04 · QA like it is live

Mobile, desktop, keyboard, motion preferences, assets, metadata, browser icons, links and edge cases get checked before the public does.

05 · Handoff or ongoing care

Take the keys with a clean handoff or keep DeeQ close for maintenance, updates and media. Ownership stays clear either way.

You bring the problem.
